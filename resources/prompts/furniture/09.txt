TASK
Compose the provided technical views of a furniture object on a single drawing sheet while maintaining professional drafting standards, correct proportions, clear dimensions, and a visually balanced layout.
SOURCE MATERIALS
The original drawing frame and title block.
Main view — front elevation.
Top view — plan.
Left-side view — left elevation.
Axonometric view.
Overall and internal dimensions of the object.
Information for completing the title block.
MAIN FRAME RULE
Use the supplied frame and title block as an entirely unmodified background.
It is strictly prohibited to:
redraw the frame;
change its dimensions or proportions;
modify the thickness, colour, or position of its lines;
rebuild or alter the title-block cells;
move or modify the logo;
crop, stretch, or scale the original frame;
cover any frame lines with white shapes or other elements.
Only the technical views may be placed within the empty drawing area, and text values may be added inside the existing title-block cells.
VIEW LAYOUT
Arrange the four views in a compact two-column composition:
upper left — FRONT ELEVATION;
upper right — LEFT ELEVATION;
lower left — TOP VIEW;
lower right — AXONOMETRIC VIEW.
The FRONT ELEVATION must be the main compositional element. Position it first and give it the most prominent location.
Place the TOP VIEW directly below the front elevation, maintaining projection alignment and the same horizontal position.
Place the LEFT ELEVATION to the right of the front elevation.
Place the AXONOMETRIC VIEW below the left elevation.
Shift the entire group of views slightly downward relative to the geometric centre of the available drawing area.
SPACING RULES
Distribute the views evenly and compactly.
The distance between adjacent views must not be greater than the distance from the outermost views to the nearest sheet border.
Do not leave an excessively large empty area in the centre of the sheet.
At the same time:
views must not touch one another;
dimension chains from adjacent views must not intersect;
sufficient clear space must remain around each view;
vertical spacing between the upper and lower rows must appear visually balanced;
lower views and captions must not be placed too close to the title block;
no element may extend beyond the usable drawing area.
SCALE
Use the same scale for all orthographic views:
FRONT ELEVATION;
LEFT ELEVATION;
TOP VIEW.
Preserve the true proportions of the object and the aspect ratio of every view. Do not stretch any view independently in the horizontal or vertical direction.
Use the largest standard drawing scale that allows all views, dimensions, and captions to fit comfortably on the sheet.
The axonometric view may use a smaller scale if it appears too large at the orthographic scale. It must remain visually secondary to the orthographic views and must not dominate the composition.
VIEW CAPTIONS
Use the following titles:
FRONT ELEVATIONLEFT ELEVATIONTOP VIEWAXONOMETRIC VIEW
The captions must:
be positioned below the corresponding views;
be aligned with the left edge of each view;
have sufficient spacing from the drawing;
use a regular, non-bold font;
use the same text height throughout;
remain visually smaller than the dimension numerals;
not include scale information;
not intersect dimension or extension lines.
DIMENSIONING RULES
Apply dimensions according to professional technical-drawing standards.
Mandatory requirements:
show each dimension only once;
place dimension lines primarily outside the object outline;
place smaller dimensions closer to the object;
place larger and overall dimensions farther from the object;
prevent dimension and extension lines from intersecting;
dimensions must not obstruct the geometry of the object;
group dimensions belonging to the same construction element;
connect every dimension to a specific edge, part, or feature;
whenever possible, place dimensions on only two sides of each view;
align dimensions with the principal axes;
in the axonometric view, dimension lines must be parallel to the corresponding object edges and axes;
dimension lines and numerals must be lighter or thinner than the object lines;
visible outlines must remain the strongest and most prominent lines;
hidden edges must be shown using thin, light dashed lines.
GRAPHIC STYLE
Use a clean white technical-drawing style:
pure white background;
no shadows;
no perspective distortion in orthographic views;
no materials, textures, or coloured fills;
no decorative elements;
no accessories or surrounding objects;
thin, sharp object lines with consistent line weight;
dimension lines thinner and lighter than object lines;
hidden edges represented by light dashed lines;
consistent graphic density and contrast across all views.
TITLE BLOCK
Do not modify the structure of the original title block. Add information only within its existing empty fields.
Use a clean font proportional to the size of each cell. Text must not touch cell borders or overlap the original field labels.
Example information:
Type of documentation: GENERAL ARRANGEMENTType of object: DISPLAY CABINETName of project: HORIZON RESIDENCEName of room: LIVING ROOMFloor: 04
SPECIFICATION
Do not add a specification, parts list, or any additional tables to the sheet.
FINAL QUALITY CHECK
Before export, confirm that:
The original frame and title block remain completely unchanged.
All orthographic views use the same scale.
The axonometric view is smaller than the orthographic views.
Every view preserves its original proportions.
The views form a compact composition without excessive empty space in the centre.
The spacing between views does not exceed the spacing between the views and the frame.
Captions are placed below the views, left-aligned, and set in a regular font.
Dimensions do not intersect the object, other dimensions, or captions.
No element overlaps the frame or title block.
The sheet appears evenly filled, professionally balanced, and easy to read.
FINAL OUTPUT
Prepare one drawing sheet using the original format and proportions of the supplied frame.
Export:
one full-resolution PNG;
one single-page PDF in the corresponding standard sheet format.
Do not modify the geometry of the technical views, the original frame, the title block, or the logo.
