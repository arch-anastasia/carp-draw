GEOMETRY REQUIREMENTS
Identify the furniture object and separate it from the background, room setting, decorations, and accessories.
Include only the furniture and its permanent components.
Build the model at a true 1:1 scale. Use millimetres for modeling parameters.
Use the following coordinate system:
X — width;
Y — depth;
Z — height.
Treat the explicitly specified overall dimensions as authoritative.
Preserve the source furniture’s silhouette, component proportions, arrangement, thicknesses, rounded edges, gaps, and joints.
Reproduce all visible components that belong to the furniture, including the carcass, panels, fronts, doors, drawers, shelves, niches, partitions, plinth, frames, guides, grooves, and integrated lighting where present.
Do not add exterior handles, hardware, compartments, or decorative elements that are not supported by the source images.
Use the photographs and line drawings to estimate component proportions. Do not present estimates from perspective images as exact measurements.
Create every structural component as a separate, clearly named object organized into logical groups.
Create actual three-dimensional geometry with real thicknesses. Do not substitute photographs on flat planes for structural geometry.
Do not distort the model to match a single camera viewpoint.
INTERIOR CONSTRUCTION
Create a complete, plausible interior appropriate to the furniture type.
For cabinet furniture, include the necessary side panels, top, bottom, back panel, and internal supports.
Each drawer must contain:
a separate drawer front;
two side walls;
a front wall;
a rear wall;
a bottom.
Where hidden construction is necessary to complete the model, independently choose the simplest reasonable solution. Preserve the visible exterior and specified overall dimensions.
Use sensible thicknesses, clearances, and component connections. Document all unavoidable assumptions separately.
Do not stop solely because hidden dimensions are unknown. Do not claim that assumed internal dimensions were accurately recovered from the photographs.
Avoid unnecessary complex hardware and unsupported mechanisms.
MOVABLE COMPONENTS
Make drawers and doors movable where present.
All components of a drawer must move together. Doors must rotate around the appropriate hinge axis.
Provide functional clearances and check for unintended intersections in the closed position and during the demonstrated opening movement.
VISUAL STYLE
Use a clean white technical line-drawing style:
background: pure white #FFFFFF;
surfaces: white, without textures or gradients;
visible edges: solid dark lines #111111;
hidden edges: light-grey dashed lines #888888;
visible lines must be thin and consistently weighted;
hidden lines must be lighter and thinner than visible lines;
shadows disabled by default;
lighting must not produce tonal shading on the white surfaces.
For the interactive viewer:
visible-line width: 0.8 px;
hidden-line width: 0.6 px;
hidden-line dash pattern: 6/5 px;
hidden-edge display enabled by default;
shadows disabled by default;
separate controls for hidden edges and shadows;
rotation and zoom controls;
controls for opening drawers or doors where present.
Determine visible and hidden edges from the actual geometry and current camera position. Do not display triangulation lines across flat surfaces.
EXPORTS
Provide:
A standalone interactive HTML preview of the actual model.
A GLB model with separately named components and materials.
An OBJ and MTL pair.
An editable parametric model, such as OpenSCAD or a Blender model accompanied by a reproducible construction script and parameter file.
A JSON manifest containing the overall dimensions, component list, units, and documented assumptions.
A ZIP archive containing all final files.
The full technical line style, including camera-dependent hidden edges, must work in the interactive HTML viewer. If an exchange format cannot preserve a display feature, retain the correct geometry and document that limitation.
Do not export orthographic views, dimensioned drawings, drawing sheets, or PNG/SVG technical drawings at this stage.
FINAL VERIFICATION
Before delivery, verify:
the overall bounding dimensions in the closed position;
true 1:1 physical scale and correct units;
correspondence with the supplied photographs and line drawings;
preservation of the exterior geometry and component arrangement;
real component thicknesses and valid solid geometry;
absence of unintended component intersections;
correct movement of drawers and doors;
correct distinction between visible and hidden edges;
consistent line weights and hidden-line dash pattern;
white surfaces without textures or tonal shading;
hidden edges enabled and shadows disabled by default;
successful reopening of the exported model with its scale and component structure preserved;
correct operation of the interactive viewer.
Keep intermediate images and diagnostic comparisons internal.
After completing the work, show the interactive 3D preview and provide links to the HTML, GLB, OBJ/MTL, editable parametric model, and complete ZIP archive.
Perform the modeling and provide the completed files. A description, an unexecuted script, or a static image alone is not sufficient.
