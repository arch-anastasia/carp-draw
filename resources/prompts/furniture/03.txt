Create an exact construction line drawing of the furniture shown in Image 1.
INPUT ROLES
Image 1 is the only source of geometry and the fixed, unchangeable coordinate reference.
Image 2 is a style reference only. Use it only for the thin, clean, precise character of the lines. Never copy, infer, or transfer geometry from Image 2.
PRIORITY ORDER
Exact geometry and pixel alignment with Image 1.
Complete representation of every visible furniture boundary and component thickness.
Thin, clean line appearance matching Image 2.
If any requirements conflict, preserve the geometry of Image 1.
GEOMETRY LOCK
Trace the furniture at exactly the same scale and coordinates as Image 1.
Preserve without modification:
the original canvas dimensions and aspect ratio;
the exact viewpoint and perspective;
the furniture’s position and surrounding margins;
the complete outer silhouette;
the proportions and dimensions of every component;
every horizontal, vertical and angled construction edge;
all perspective angles and minor geometric irregularities;
all openings, recesses, shelves, panels, drawer fronts, supports and base components;
every joint, corner, return, end, overlap and depth edge.
Do not crop, resize, recenter, rotate, straighten, normalize, symmetrize, simplify, redesign or convert the furniture into an orthographic projection.
VISIBLE CONSTRUCTION BOUNDARIES
Trace every visible physical construction boundary and only physical construction boundaries.
Carefully include:
both sides of every visible panel edge;
every visible slab, shelf and panel thickness;
narrow edge bands, returns and chamfers when they represent real geometry;
the complete top perimeter;
all visible boundaries inside top openings and recesses;
internal walls, rims, rails and depth transitions;
upper and lower edges of shelves;
shelf front thicknesses and side returns;
rear shelf junctions and internal perspective edges;
vertical partitions, supports and jamb thicknesses;
drawer and front-panel seams;
side-panel corner joints;
all visible base or plinth boundaries and end returns.
Whenever two visible parallel boundaries describe the thickness of one component, retain both lines. Never collapse a component thickness into a single line.
Do not trace:
wood grain or material texture;
shadows, highlights, reflections or lighting transitions;
background edges;
decorative photographic noise;
accessories or non-furniture objects;
boundaries created only by changes in color or illumination.
OCCLUSION RULE
Show only construction boundaries that are actually visible in Image 1.
Do not reconstruct hidden parts, extend lines behind overlapping components or invent missing geometry based on how furniture would normally be constructed.
If a non-furniture object covers a furniture boundary, remove the object from the output but leave the covered boundary absent. Do not draw through the occlusion.
If a visible photographic boundary is soft or blurred, place one clean line along the geometric center of that boundary.
LINE APPEARANCE
Match the line character of Image 2:
pure white background: RGB 255, 255, 255;
pure black lines: RGB 0, 0, 0;
uniformly thin 1-pixel strokes;
crisp, solid and continuous lines within every visible segment;
identical line thickness throughout;
precise technical linework, not sketch-like linework.
No fills, gray pixels, colors, shading, gradients, hatching, textures, antialias haze, loose marks, labels, dimensions, arrows, text, signatures or watermarks.

every visible component thickness;
all drawer and panel seams;

FINAL OUTPUT
Return only one final PNG image.
Use exactly the same pixel dimensions and aspect ratio as Image 1. Do not provide explanations, previews, alternative versions, borders, captions or additional content.
